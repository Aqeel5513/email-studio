**How often does a box arrive?**
Once a month, by post. The club page has the delivery details.

**I mostly drink tea bags. Is this for me?**
Very much so. The brewing card in each box tells you how much tea to use, how hot the water should be and how long to steep. If you can boil a kettle, you're ready.

**Can I give The Steep Club as a gift?**
Yes. You can send a box to someone else, and they'll get a new set of teas each month.

**Do I choose the teas?**
We choose them for you, so each box brings something new to try. If a tea isn't for you, tell us. We read every note.

**Can I pause or cancel?**
The club page sets out exactly how pausing and cancelling work, so you can read the details before you join.
