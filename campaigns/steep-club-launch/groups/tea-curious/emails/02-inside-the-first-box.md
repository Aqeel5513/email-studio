If you're just starting out with loose-leaf tea, we thought you might like to see what's inside a Steep Club box before you decide. So let's lift the lid.

**A few teas to get to know.** Each month we choose teas that sit well together. Some will feel familiar. Others will be brand new to you, like a tea with a toasty, nutty taste or one that smells of fresh flowers before you've even poured it.

**Notes on every tea.** For each one, we tell you where it comes from and what to notice as you sip. It's a little like a postcard from somewhere new.

**A brewing card.** How much tea, how hot the water, how long to steep. Short and clear, so you can get a good cup the first time.

We put the box together slowly and carefully, and we hope you'll open it the same way. Make a cup, try something new, and see what you like.
