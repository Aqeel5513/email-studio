Since the club opened, we've been thinking about what you might want to know if loose-leaf tea is new to you. Here are a few questions you might be wondering about.

**How often does a box arrive?**
Once a month, by post. The club page has the delivery details.

**I mostly drink tea bags. Is this for me?**
Very much so. The brewing card in each box tells you how much tea to use, how hot the water should be and how long to steep. If you can boil a kettle, you're ready to start exploring.

**Can I give The Steep Club as a gift?**
Yes. The club page explains the gift options and how the gift reaches the person you're giving it to.

**Do I choose the teas?**
We choose them for you, so each box brings something new to try. If a tea isn't for you, we'd love to hear what you think.

**Can I pause or cancel?**
The club page sets out exactly how pausing and cancelling work, so you can read the details before you join.
