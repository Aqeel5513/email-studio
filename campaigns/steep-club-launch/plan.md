# The Steep Club launch: campaign plan

Kettlebird Tea, email to our waitlist only.
Campaign runs Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks).

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** The Kettle's On

**In one sentence:** We invite the people on our waitlist to join The Steep Club, our monthly tea box, with a short run of warm, unhurried emails that show them what's inside and let them decide in their own time.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.

That is 15% of a 2,000-person list. It's a stretch for email alone, but realistic for people who chose to join a waitlist. The halfway check (section 7) tells us early if we're on track.

**Other goals:**

- Keep unsubscribes under 1% per email, so the list stays healthy for future boxes.
- Sell at least 40 gift subscriptions within the 300.
- Learn which group responds most, to shape next month's emails.

---

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people who joined The Steep Club waitlist. They already raised their hand, so they are at the **decision** stage: they don't need convincing that tea is nice, they need a clear picture of the box and an easy way to say yes.

We don't yet know the exact size of each group. Until the lead list is ready, this plan assumes roughly a third in each (about 667 people).

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might hold them back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | Like tea, mostly drink bags, want to explore | A friendly guide, simple brewing help, no snobbery | Worry it's "too fancy" for them, or that they'll brew it wrong |
| **Gift buyers** | Shopping for a tea lover in their life, often near the holidays | An easy, thoughtful gift that feels personal | Timing, not knowing the person's taste, how the gift is handed over |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Drink tea every day, already know what they like | New teas worth their time, details on origin and taste | Already have a cupboard full, wonder if it's better than what they buy now |

**Where they are:** their inbox. This campaign is email only, so every touch happens there.

---

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Once a month, The Steep Club brings you new teas to steep, sip and get to know, so you can slow down with something new.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **Discover something new each month.** Each box brings teas you may not have tried, with notes on where they come from and how they taste.
   *Proof:* the teas chosen for the first box and their tasting notes. *(To confirm with the team before writing.)*
2. **Easy for anyone to brew.** Every box comes with a simple brewing card: how much tea, how hot, how long.
   *Proof:* a photo or sample of the brewing card. *(To confirm.)*
3. **A gift that keeps arriving.** A subscription gives someone a small, calm moment every month, not just once.
   *Proof:* gift options and how the gift is delivered. *(To confirm.)*
4. **Join at your own pace.** No pressure to decide today. Members can pause or cancel when they like.
   *Proof:* the actual pause and cancel terms. *(To confirm before we say this.)*

**Message by group:**

| Group | Lead with | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | Messages 2 and 1 | Encouraging, "you don't need to be an expert" |
| Gift buyers | Message 3 | Thoughtful, practical, helpful |
| Daily tea drinkers | Message 1, with real origin and taste detail | Curious, a little more in-depth |

**What we leave out:** prices are not in this plan. They go in the emails from the shop's product page. We say how a tea tastes, smells and feels to drink. We make no health or medical promises and no "we're the top tea" claims. No countdowns, no pressure.

## 4. Channel strategy

**Email is the only channel.** It fits well: the audience already gave us their email and asked to hear about the club, and it costs almost nothing to send.

| Part of email | Why it fits | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch emails to everyone | One shared moment to open the club | Short letter-style email, one clear link to join | Low |
| Group-specific emails | Each group has a different reason to join | Same date, three versions, different lead message | Medium |
| Welcome email for new members | Confirms the order and starts the relationship | Triggered automatically when someone subscribes | Low |
| Remove new members from launch emails | Nobody should be asked to join after they've joined | List rule, checked before every send | Low |

**Not used:** paid ads, social posts, PR or partners. They could be added in a later month if email falls short.

## 7. Success metrics

**Main KPI:** 300 new subscriptions from the waitlist by Sunday 3 January 2027.

Rough path to 300:

| Point in time | Target total | Why |
|---|---|---|
| End of week 1 (Sun 13 Dec) | 120 | Launch emails bring the most eager people |
| Halfway check (Fri 18 Dec) | 150 | Below 110 here means we change course (see section 9) |
| End of week 3 (Sun 27 Dec) | 240 | Gift buyers peak before the holidays |
| End of week 4 (Sun 3 Jan) | 300 | Quiet-week note brings in the slower deciders |

Rough split by group (assumes equal group sizes, to update once the lead list is ready): daily drinkers 120, beginners 100, gift buyers 80.

**Other KPIs:**

| Metric | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Click rate per email | 5% or more | Clicks on the join link, from the email tool |
| Click-to-subscribe rate | 25% or more | Subscriptions divided by clicks |
| Unsubscribe rate | Under 1% per email | Email tool |
| Gift subscriptions | 40 or more | Orders marked as gifts |
| Subscriptions by group | See split above | Tag each link with the group name |

Open rates are a rough guide only, because some email apps report them unreliably.

**Reporting:** a short count every Monday during the campaign, the halfway check on Fri 18 Dec, and a full write-up on Fri 8 Jan 2027.

## 8. Budget

The budget is small and the campaign is email only, so most of the cost is time, not money.

| Item | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Writing and checking 8 emails (10 versions, counting email 01's three) plus the welcome email | Production (time) | The largest cost. Reuse one structure across versions |
| Brewing card and printable gift card | Production (time, maybe a small print or design cost) | Can be simple, made in-house |
| Join page updates | Production (time) | Uses the existing shop |
| Email sending | Distribution | Free or low cost at 2,000 contacts |
| Contingency | About 10-15% of any money spent | Covers an extra email or design fix if needed |

No money goes to paid ads.

---

## 9. Risks and what we'll do about them

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **We're behind at the halfway check** (under 110 by Fri 18 Dec) | Look at which group is lagging and send that group one extra helpful email (for example, a tasting-notes email for daily drinkers). Keep it helpful, never pushy |
| **The holidays bury our emails** (24 December to 1 January) | Send nothing on 24 and 25 December. Make the 29 December email short, calm and easy to read on a phone |
| **Emails start to feel like pressure** (unsubscribes over 1%) | Drop the next email for that group, and check each draft against the voice guide before it goes |
| **Someone who already joined gets asked to join** | Remove new members before every send and check the list on the practice server first |
| **Proof points aren't ready in time** | Write around what is confirmed. Don't promise a tea, a term or a feature we haven't checked |
