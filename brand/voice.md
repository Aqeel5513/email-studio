# Kettlebird Tea brand voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, and we write to people who joined its waitlist. Use this guide for every email we write.

## Our voice in three words

- **Warm.** Write like a friend who is glad to hear from the reader. Be kind and personal, never stiff or corporate.
- **Curious.** Share what's interesting about a tea: where it comes from, how it tastes, how to brew it. Invite the reader to try something new.
- **Unhurried.** Give the reader time. No pressure, no countdowns, no rushing them to decide.

## Words we use

- steep
- sip
- slow down
- the kettle's on

Use them where they fit naturally. Don't cram them all into one email.

## Words we avoid

- hurry
- act now
- limited time
- game-changer

These sound pushy or salesy and go against our unhurried voice. Avoid close variations too, such as "don't miss out" or "last chance".

## Claims we must never make

- That our tea **detoxes your body**.
- That our tea **cures stress**, or cures or treats anything else.
- That we sell the **best tea in the world**, or any other "best" we can't prove.

We can say how a tea tastes, smells and feels to drink. We don't make health or medical promises, and we don't make claims we can't back up.

## How we sign off

Every email ends with:

> Until the next cup,
> The Kettlebird team
